About this app

This tool classifies short reviews as positive or negative. Text is tokenized and mapped to a Doc2Vec vector, then a k-nearest neighbors classifier (k=9) picks the label. The model is trained on thousands of labelled sentences from review sites (cached locally after the first run).

How to use it

  1. Wait until the status shows the model is ready.
  2. Enter or paste a review, or click an example chip.
  3. Click Analyze sentiment to see the label and scores.

Runs entirely on this server — your text is not sent to a third-party API.
